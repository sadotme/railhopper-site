RailHopper

Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

RailHopper is a mine cart arcade game for iPhone, made by Steven Avila. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

What the game collects

Nothing. RailHopper has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network connections and works entirely offline.

What stays on your phone

The game saves a few small values in your iPhone's local storage so it can remember them between plays:

  • your best distance in Endless mode and in each day's Daily tunnel,
  • whether you last played Endless or Daily,
  • whether you've already seen the "Lit TNT" tip.

These never leave your device and are not sent to me or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

RailHopper collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds online leaderboards, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
